# Research Sources

The isolated strategy scan is based on these research threads.

1. Cooper, Cliff, and Gulen, "Return Differences between Trading and
   Non-Trading Hours: Like Night and Day."
   https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract_id=1004081

   Motivation: decompose returns into open-to-close and close-to-open windows;
   test overnight long bias and opening fade behavior.

2. Gao, Han, Li, and Zhou, "Market Intraday Momentum."
   https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S0304405X18301351

   Motivation: first-half-hour returns can predict last-half-hour returns; test
   last-half-hour market timing rules.

3. Moskowitz, Ooi, and Pedersen, "Time Series Momentum."
   https://pages.stern.nyu.edu/~lpederse/papers/TimeSeriesMomentum.pdf

   Motivation: an asset's own past returns predict future returns across equity
   index, currency, commodity, and bond futures; test short-horizon variants on
   available data.

4. Heston, Korajczyk, and Sadka, "Intraday Patterns in the Cross-Section of
   Stock Returns."
   https://ideas.repec.org/a/bla/jfinan/v65y2010i4p1369-1407.html

   Motivation: intraday return continuation at repeated half-hour intervals;
   test session-window continuation rules.

5. Indriawan, Lien, Wen, and Xu, crude oil intraday return predictability / EIA
   announcement thread.
   https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract_id=3822093

   Motivation: crude oil first-half-hour and announcement-window returns can
   predict the last half hour; test crude-specific variants.

6. FX intraday momentum literature, including ruble-dollar explicit trading-hour
   evidence.
   https://biblio.ugent.be/publication/8060014

   Motivation: test London and NY session momentum/fade rules on available FX
   pairs.
